Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Come Taiwan

Does Come Taiwan work completely offline?

Yes! All core guides, subway maps, local neural AI answers, currency calculations, and audio pronunciation cards work 100% offline without cellular roaming or Wi-Fi.

Do I need to create an account or provide personal data?

No. You can use the app immediately after download without any registration. We do not track your location history or sell your personal data.

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone?

Open the app, tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top right corner, and select "Restore Purchases (恢復購買)". Your PRO privileges will be restored automatically via Apple StoreKit.

How do I change the display language?

Tap the Language Selector at the top right of any screen or go to Settings to switch between all 11 supported languages anytime.
